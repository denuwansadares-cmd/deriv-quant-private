# Roadmap and Support/SLA Options

## Value-priority roadmap

1. Obtain clean dataset licences, chain-of-title signatures, and current API-terms legal memo.
2. Produce locked, buyer-reproducible holdout/ablation evidence with no strategy changes after lock.
3. Complete second broker adapter against the existing interface.
4. Commission independent security, code, quant, and risk audits.
5. Complete 72-hour real-time soak and process-level network chaos evidence.
6. Add signed releases, provenance attestation, Vault/keychain and deployment hardening.
7. Add strategy SDK, experiment UI, charts, report signing and authenticated multi-user API.
8. Establish paying customers or a strategic distribution partnership.

## Illustrative support options (not contractual offers)

| Tier | Coverage | Target response |
|---|---|---|
| Handover | build/evaluation questions for 30 days | 2 business days |
| Standard | fixes, dependency updates, quarterly evidence refresh | 1 business day |
| Enterprise | agreed hours, deployment support, incident channel | contract-defined |

Availability, fees, service credits, security duties, exclusions, time zone, and termination require a signed SLA. No uptime or financial-performance SLA is currently offered.
