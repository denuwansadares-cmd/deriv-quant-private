# Data Provenance and Licences

No external historical market dataset is included. Synthetic test fixtures are original/generated solely for tests. `HISTORY_PATH` is a loader capability, not evidence of data ownership.

A transferable dataset schedule must record: dataset ID/checksum, supplier, acquisition date, licence version, permitted purposes, retention, redistribution/assignment right, territory, personal-data status, deletion process, and evidence location.

Raw API content and seller credentials are excluded from delivery. API-derived metadata remains subject to provider terms and legal review. Locked holdout results are absent because no independently licensed holdout dataset has been supplied.
