# Data Provenance and Licences

No external historical market dataset is included in the data-room package. Synthetic test fixtures are original/generated solely for tests. `HISTORY_PATH` loads operator-held data without bundling it into a source delivery.

On 2026-08-06 the operator reported a direct confirmation from Deriv support that the historical data is provided for long-term retention and does not require a separate licence for that retention. Preserve the original support conversation (including case/reference ID if available) as evidence. This confirmation supports local storage, research, and backtesting; it is not recorded as permission to redistribute or transfer the raw dataset.

A transferable dataset schedule must record: dataset ID/checksum, supplier, acquisition date, licence version, permitted purposes, retention, redistribution/assignment right, territory, personal-data status, deletion process, and evidence location.

Raw API content and seller credentials are excluded from delivery. API-derived metadata remains subject to provider terms and legal review. Locked holdout results have not yet been produced from the retained dataset.
